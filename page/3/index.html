Update 2026/8/4

A while back (about two or three weeks ago now), I did a major overhaul of this website. All the content on the site now supports English, Malay Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese.

This was pretty massive undertaking that burned through my entire month’s worth of token in one go. The Hexo theme I’m using doesn’t support multiple languages, so I basically forced this feature into my site by brute force. Now you don’t even need to check the source code on Github to imagine how terrifying it must look, right? Hehehe~

Although building a multilingual site is a lot of thankless effort, when I really thought about it: I’m Malaysian, and if I only used one language, there’s no way I could reach everyone I think might possibly read my blog. Of course I don’t actually expect my blog to have readers, but would I be missing out on a chance for people to get to know me because of that?

In terms of coverage, technically just Traditional Chinese and English would be enough at the bare minimum. But… since I was already to take a knife to the whole site, why not just add a few more languages while I’m at it?

Traditional Chinese: Chinese is, at the end of the day, my mother tongue: the language I can express myself best in. I usually write articles in Chinese first and then translate them into other languages. Also, since I frequently attend BlogBlogParty, most of this blog’s current readers are probably Taiwanese, who are more used to reading Traditional Chinese.

Simplified Chinese: But I’m a Malaysian Chinese, and most of my local friends mainly use Simplified Chinese. Everyone can read Traditional Chinese, but that doesn’t mean they enjoy it. Converting Traditional Chinese to Simplified Chinese is very easy anyway, since it’s the same language. I can just use an off-the-shelf converter.

English: Whether I end up studying in Malaysia or abroad in the future, English will be the main language during my university years, and there’s a good chance I’ll be immersed in English-speaking circles for a long time. So I figured it’s better to plan ahead.

Malay: If I want to discuss very local topics (politics?) and current events, I think expressing myself in Malay would flow more naturally than in Chinese. Again, I want to reach as many people as possible, at least within Malaysia. And of course, most importantly, I just enjoy doing it, so I’m willing to put in the effort.

The Cost

But the cost of doing this is pretty significant. Every time I publish a post, I now need to rewrite it in English in Malay as well, and 2-3 hours has become the baseline production time just for one article. But as I’ve mentioned before, writing articles has always been extremely time-consuming and difficult for me, so I’ve already gotten used to the feeling that every article is a big project. As for older posts, I’ve already translated them into Simplified Chinese; O’ll fill in the English and Malay versions whenever I get the chance later.

Afterword

After finishing this overhaul of the site, I find myself feeling a bit regretful again: the site has gotten so bloated now. Either way, I can live with it, and honestly kind of like it, because it really suits my personality (much like my perpetually messy desk). But whether readers can live with it is a whole other matter. I believe all this fuss was worth it.

Wes Mangan?

説起 Java，大部分人都會想到程式語言，或者是Minecraft。但在世界的另一端，在硅谷的背面，爪哇語（Basa Jawa/Javanese） 是印尼一門超過8000萬人使用的語言哦！

爪哇語主要由爪哇人使用。雖然我居住在馬來西亞，但身邊不少馬來朋友都是爪哇裔後代，便多多少少接觸到爪哇文化了。
Wes mangan，在爪哇語中是呷飽沒的意思，恭喜你學會了你的第一句爪哇話啦~

滅絕危機

雖然爪哇語擁有8000萬使用者，但它不是任何國家的官方語言！這是爲什麽呢？
在1945年印尼獨立運動之時，開國總統蘇加諾擔下了要統一全國的重任。統一的第一步，就是要統一語言。

但是印尼，可是擁有17000個島嶼，700種語言，1945年就有7400萬人口的超複雜國家。如果選用接近一半人口使用的語言——爪哇語，那麽非爪哇族的人肯定會超不爽，憑什麽少數要服從多數？爲了防止種族衝突，蘇加諾做出了驚人的決定——使用當時僅有超少人用的語言——廖内馬來語設爲國語。由於對所有人來説都是外語，反而達成了公平。從此，爪哇語就失去官方地位了……

冷知識1：猶如 Java 中公私分明的函數一樣，爪哇語也有一套階級系統。爪哇語主要分爲三個主要階層：

  • Ngoko（粗語）用於同齡人、同輩對晚輩
  • Madya（中性語）用於陌生人
  • Krama（高雅語）用於晚輩對長輩、下級對上級
    這三種階層的爪哇語，可是連基本詞匯都不一樣，所以我認爲會爪哇語的人都超强，相當於一次學了三門語言。例如“吃”這個字，對朋友說是“mangan”，對長輩必須用“dahar”；“你”對朋友使用是“kowe”，對長輩使用是“panjenengan”。想用爪哇語聊天，你必須時刻掂量對方的身份，用錯一個詞匯都是極大的冒犯。這種見人説人話，見鬼說鬼話的體系，實在是太繁雜了，也是爪哇語沒有成爲印尼國語的原因之一。

冷知識2：新加坡將英語設爲核心行政與工作語言，是爲了平衡三大民族之間的隔閡，亦是同理。

Java 的音樂

甘美蘭（Gamelan，意為合奏）是爪哇人的傳統樂器，也是印尼歷史最悠久的樂器。甘美蘭的音調和西方樂器完全不同，并沒有統一的標準音調，因此世界上沒有任何兩個音準相同的甘美蘭。

冷知識：徳布西在1889年的巴黎博覽會時，爪哇甘美蘭給了他巨大的啓發，在他的作品 Estampes 中的 Pagodes 樂章尤爲明顯。

那麽 Java（程式語言）爲什麽會叫 Java 呢？

起初，創始人原本想要把它命名爲“Oak”，因爲創始人門口正對著一顆橡樹。然而，在准備商業化推廣時，發現Oak這個名字早就被注冊了，被迫改名。此時，又因爲創作團隊人們對一種來自爪哇島上的咖啡情有獨鍾，便決定將之命名爲Java。
